Transparent Cancellation & Refund Policy

1. Free Cancellation Window

For all InCity, Intercity, Airport, and Rental bookings, cancellation initiated at least 2 hours prior to scheduled pickup time attracts ZERO penalty. 100% of advance paid amount is refunded immediately to the original payment source.

2. Late Cancellation

If cancellation is requested within 2 hours of scheduled pickup and a chauffeur has already been dispatched, a nominal dispatch allowance of ₹250 is deducted, and the balance amount is refunded.

3. Refund Processing Timeline

UPI and Debit card refunds reflect instantaneously or within 2 hours. Credit card and NetBanking refunds typically reflect within 2 to 4 banking working days depending on issuing bank.
