Transparent Cancellation & Refund Policy

1. Free Cancellation Window

For all InCity, Intercity, Airport, and Rental bookings, cancellation initiated at least 2 hours prior to scheduled pickup time attracts ZERO penalty. 100% of advance paid amount is refunded immediately to the original payment source.

2. Late Cancellation

If cancellation is requested within 2 hours of scheduled pickup and a chauffeur has already been dispatched, a nominal dispatch allowance of ₹250 is deducted, and the balance amount is refunded.

3. Refund Processing Timeline

UPI and Debit card refunds reflect instantaneously or within 2 hours. Credit card and NetBanking refunds typically reflect within 2 to 4 banking working days depending on issuing bank.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Which vehicle models are accepted for fleet attachment? +
We accept yellow-board commercial T-permit vehicles manufactured in 2017 or later in clean, well-maintained condition. Categories include Compact Sedans (Dzire, Etios, Aura), Prime SUVs (Ertiga, XL6, Carens), Premium Executive SUVs (Innova Crysta), and Tempo Travellers.
How and when are payments disbursed to partners? +
Trip earnings are calculated with transparent, low platform fees. Automated payouts are processed every Tuesday directly to your registered bank account via NEFT/IMPS with downloadable GST invoices. Instant on-demand payouts are also available via UPI.
Can I attach my cars and hire drivers to operate them? +
Yes! Our partner dashboard is purpose-built for fleet owners. You can register multiple drivers, assign them to specific cars, swap drivers anytime, and monitor live vehicle locations and daily driver collections remotely.
What documents are required to complete onboarding? +
You will need: (1) Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC), (2) Commercial Tourist Permit & Fitness Certificate, (3) Comprehensive Commercial Vehicle Insurance, and (4) Owner/Partner PAN, Aadhaar, and GSTIN (if registered). For drivers: Valid Commercial DL and Aadhaar card.
